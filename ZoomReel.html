Layers

This tool draws on

Each tool remembers its own choice: Brush and Text start on Artwork, Arrow and Circle on Guides. The chip on the canvas always shows where the next mark will go. Hidden layers are left out of presentations, videos and PNG frames.

Fill

Click inside a shape drawn with the brush, arrows or circles. Small breaks in the outline up to this size are bridged. The fill is placed behind your lines and uses the current colour and opacity. Click an existing fill to recolour it.

Arrows and circles

Drag to draw. Shift snaps arrows to 45° and makes a true circle; Option draws a circle from its centre. Tap an arrow or circle with the Arrow, Circle or Pan tool, or double-click it with Select, to fly to what it points at or holds.

Motion
Swings from

Boil redraws lines with a small wobble a few times a second, like hand-drawn animation; images and text shiver. Sway rocks the whole selection around one point, so a drawing moves as one. Both play in the editor, presenting and videos.

Option-click (or Option-drag a box) to pick single items inside a drawing or group. Depth is the zoom level the selection belongs to. Going deeper shrinks it in place, around its own centre, so it can nest inside what's around it. Grouped objects change depth together. Arrows nudge by a tenth of its size.

Images land in the middle of the current view at half its width, so zoom to the depth you want first. You can also drop images onto the canvas. In the list, the pencil opens settings and the eye hides.

On the canvas

Everything you draw at one zoom level becomes one drawing; change the zoom and the next mark starts a new one. Option-click an item on the canvas to pick it out of its drawing.

Frame
Stops

Frame a view inside the orange corners, then add it as a stop. The pencil opens a stop's arrival and hold settings; the tick closes them. The eye hides a stop so playback and videos skip it. Drag the handle to reorder.

View

These move the camera, not your objects: turning the view never changes the artwork. To turn a drawing or image, select it and use Rotate in the Draw tab.
